## Cluster A — 3D Worlds & WebGL
*Reach for this cluster when the brief is: immersive, spatial, sci-fi, game-adjacent, WebGL-first*

**Star Atlas**
- Techniques: Three.js particle nebula, bloom/glow via additive blending, floating ambient assets, slow orbital animation
- DNA: Deep space, navy/purple, ethereal glow, game UI overlay
- Steal: Fibonacci sphere particle distribution + additive blending = instant cosmic depth without EffectComposer

**Bruno Simon Portfolio**
- Techniques: Three.js + cannon.js physics, pointer-controlled vehicle, interactive desk scene, warm 3D lighting
- DNA: Playful toy world, warm amber, physics-reactive objects
- Steal: Making 3D feel joyful through physics interaction — the experience IS the navigation

**Dunkirk WebVR**
- Techniques: WebGL 360° environment, exponential fog for atmosphere, ambient audio sync, pointer-based look
- DNA: WWII cinematic, desaturated, tense, documentary
- Steal: Heavy fog + dark palette = immersive depth even without complex geometry

**Active Theory v4**
- Techniques: WebGL shader distortion on hover (displacement mapping), custom cursor system, parallax 3D layers, GSAP smooth scroll
- DNA: Dark creative agency, monochrome + electric neon, high craft
- Steal: WebGL image distortion on hover — ripple/displacement that makes imagery feel fluid and tactile

**Persepolis Reimagined**
- Techniques: Three.js illustrated 3D world, GSAP scroll-driven camera movement, SVG/3D hybrid approach
- DNA: Iranian cultural heritage, hand-drawn-meets-3D, warm terracotta palette
- Steal: Three.js for illustrated/artistic scenes — not product visualization but world-building

**Prometheus Fuels**
- Techniques: Three.js product visualization, dramatic three-point lighting, scroll-driven camera animation
- DNA: Clean energy, dark industrial blue, electric precision
- Steal: Simple geometry + excellent lighting far outperforms complex geometry + flat lighting

**Igloo Inc**
- Techniques: Three.js animated environment as background layer, smooth scroll foreground, corporate UI overlay
- DNA: Enterprise SaaS elevated, dark professional, sophisticated restraint
- Steal: Three.js as ambient background behind readable content — immersive without competing with UI

## Cross-Cluster Patterns
*These techniques appear across 3+ clusters — they are infrastructure, not features*

| Pattern | Appears in | Note |
|---------|-----------|------|
| Custom cursor | A, F, G (Active Theory, Nixon, Noomo, KPR) | Non-negotiable for any agency/creative brief |
| Grain/noise overlay | C, E (Blacknegative, Because Recollection, Falter, Other Side) | Instant texture; 1 CSS property + animated offset |
| Clip-path reveal | B, D, F, G (Orano, In Pieces, Nixon, Noomo) | Versatile; hover + scroll variants both essential |
| Smooth scroll | Universal | Lenis-style inertia. Non-negotiable for premium feel. |
| Dark palette + single accent | A, E, G dominant | Safest starting point for premium/creative work |
| Split text entrance | B, C, F across | Characters or words — the handshake of creative sites |
| Additive blending (Three.js) | A | Makes particles glow without post-processing overhead |
